Technology Evolution and Social Change

This project explores how digital technologies have changed communication, education, work, culture, consumption and social relationships.

General objective

To develop a simple website about technology evolution and its effects on society, applying web development and interdisciplinary knowledge.

Digital responsibility

Technology can expand access to information and opportunities, but responsible use requires attention to privacy, inclusion, sustainability and reliable information.

Education

Digital tools can support learning when they are appropriate to the context and used to complement human interaction.

Sustainability

Responsible technology includes extending device lifetimes, reducing unnecessary consumption and disposing of electronic waste correctly.

Technology changes quickly, but people and society remain at the center of every technological transformation.
